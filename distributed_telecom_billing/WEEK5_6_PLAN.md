# Assignment 3 — Weeks 5 & 6

The implementation details, run instructions, experimental method and evidence checklist are maintained in `ASSIGNMENT3_WEEK5_6_STEP_BY_STEP.md`.

- Week 5: durable 2PC, ACID mapping, coordinator journal, abort/commit decision recovery, participant consistency and durability checks.
- Week 6: race conditions, mutex protection, resource-allocation/wait-for graphs, deadlock detection and victim recovery, ordered-lock prevention, contention and starvation indicators.
- Docker Compose: separate billing, rating and ledger processes with persistent SQLite volumes.
- Apache Spark: batch aggregation of Week 5 and Week 6 CSV experiment outputs.
